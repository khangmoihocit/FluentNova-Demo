12` Design System Narrative

## Visual Identity & Color Philosophy
This design system utilizes a vibrant and energetic palette defined by a striking primary orange (#FF8C42). This color is the cornerstone of the brand's identity, used for key calls-to-action and primary interactive elements to drive engagement. To provide professional balance, a deep sky blue (#1E88E5) serves as the secondary color, offering a clear contrast for secondary actions and supporting UI elements. The interface sits upon a warm, inviting neutral background (#FFF3E0), creating a soft, approachable aesthetic that avoids the starkness of pure white.

## Typography
The system employs a dual-font strategy to balance character with functionality:
- **Headlines:** We use **Manrope**, a modern geometric sans-serif that provides a clean, professional, and contemporary look for high-level information.
- **Body & Labels:** **Inter** is utilized for all functional text and body copy. Its high legibility and neutral tone ensure that content remains readable across all screen sizes and densities.

## Shape and Form
The interface adopts a high-energy, friendly appearance through the use of **maximum roundedness**. Corners are fully rounded or pill-shaped (Level 3), creating a soft, organic feel that minimizes visual tension and makes the software feel more like a tactile, modern consumer product.

## Spacing and Layout
The layout logic follows a **Normal (Level 2)** spacing scale. This provides a standard, balanced distribution of white space that ensures clear information hierarchy without the extreme airiness of an editorial layout or the density of a data-heavy application. This balance allows for efficient navigation while maintaining enough room for the rounded UI elements to breathe.
